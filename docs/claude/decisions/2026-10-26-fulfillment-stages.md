# Fulfillment stages are switched on per tenant

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Fulfillment
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** New and Delivered / Picked up are always on. Picking, Picked, Loaded and Out for delivery can each be switched on or off by the tenant.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Fulfillment stages.

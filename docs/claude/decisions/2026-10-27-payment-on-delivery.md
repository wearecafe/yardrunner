# Drivers can collect payment on delivery

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-27
Area: Payments
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Drivers collect payment on COD stops through the Collect payment on delivery permission: a Stripe Reader S710 with cellular assigned to the truck, a third-party terminal recorded by hand, or cash if the tenant allows it. Drivers see the amount due only. Not built yet.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Payment on delivery.

# Stripe Tax calculates sales tax

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Tax
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Stripe Tax is the tax engine: Ontario HST and Quebec GST + QST, tax by where the order is delivered or picked up, tax-exempt orgs with exemption numbers.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Decisions (Tax engine); `docs/claude/stripe.md`.

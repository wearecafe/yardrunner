# Orgs buy on account up to a credit limit

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Receivables
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Orgs have credit limits. Any amount above the limit needs a credit card, unless a user with Override credit limit applies an override.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Decisions (Card vs on-account).

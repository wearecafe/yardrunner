# Pallet usage fees are a second, non-refundable charge

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-27
Area: Pallets
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** A pallet usage fee is configured per tenant or yard with its own on/off and amount; each product chooses whether it carries one. It isn't refunded when the pallet returns, except when unused product comes back on full pallets. One amount per tenant or yard, no per-product override. Taxed the same as the product.

**Turned down:** A per-product amount.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Pallet deposits and pallet usage fees.

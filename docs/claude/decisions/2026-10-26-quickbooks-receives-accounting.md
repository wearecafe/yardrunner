# QuickBooks Online receives the accounting

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: QuickBooks
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** QuickBooks Online receives goods invoices, rental invoices, payments, credit notes and customer balances. YardRunner keeps no general ledger of its own.

**Turned down:** Built-in accounting (as CAVU does).

**Source:** `docs/claude/competitive-research.md` → 4b; `docs/claude/quickbooks.md`.

# Selling units can be switched off, and products round to units for sale

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Units
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Each selling unit can be switched off for sale. Each product chooses to refuse, round up or round down a quantity that doesn't fit, to the nearest mix of units for sale; staff confirm the mix before it's added.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Decisions (Units not sold on their own).

# Tenants can require a quality-check sign-off

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Fulfillment
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** A tenant can require a sign-off at Picked, Loaded or both, chooses which roles can sign off for each stage, and whether the signer must be someone other than the picker (default on). Every sign-off and rejection is logged.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Quality check sign-off.

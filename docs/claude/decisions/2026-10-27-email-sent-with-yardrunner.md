# "Sent with YardRunner" is on by default

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-27
Area: Emails
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Customer emails carry a "Sent with YardRunner" line. Only YardRunner staff can turn it off, per tenant, in the back office. It isn't tied to a plan.

**Turned down:** Tying it to a plan or add-on.

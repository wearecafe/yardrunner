# Pallet deposits are configurable per tenant and yard

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Pallets
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Each tenant or yard chooses whether to charge pallet deposits and sets the amount per pallet; each product chooses whether it carries one. Deposits are credited automatically when the pallet comes back.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Pallet deposits and pallet usage fees.

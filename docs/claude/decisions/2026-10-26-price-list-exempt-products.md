# Products can be exempt from price lists

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Pricing
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** A product flagged exempt always sells at its base price: no price list, tier, contract price, volume break or category rule applies. Manual discounts on it need the Discount exempt products permission and are always logged with who applied or approved them, the original price and the reason.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Price list rules; Manual discounts on exempt products.

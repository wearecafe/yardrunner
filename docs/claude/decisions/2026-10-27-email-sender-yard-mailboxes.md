# Customer emails go from the yard's mailboxes once verified

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-27
Area: Emails
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Customer emails go from YardRunner's address under the yard's name by default. Once the yard's domain is verified, every customer email (statements and reminders included) goes from the yard's own mailboxes.

**Source:** `docs/claude/email-branding.md` → Decisions.

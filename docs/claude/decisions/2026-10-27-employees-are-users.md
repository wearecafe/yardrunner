# Every employee is a user

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-27
Area: Time and attendance
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Team → Employees picks an existing user or adds one in the same form; the home yard must be one of the user's yards. Ending employment archives the employee and keeps the user; deactivating a user ends their employment and keeps their hours. No job titles.

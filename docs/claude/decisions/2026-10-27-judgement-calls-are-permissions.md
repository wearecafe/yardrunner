# Cross-role judgement calls are permissions with role defaults

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-27
Area: Access
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** Credit overrides, manual line prices, exempt-product discounts, refunds above the limit, cost visibility, P&L access and timesheet approval are separate permissions with role defaults, granted per user and logged.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Permissions.

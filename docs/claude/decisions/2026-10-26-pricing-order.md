# Contract price, then price list, then base price

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Pricing
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** For each line: an exempt product sells at its base price; otherwise a contract price agreed with the org wins; otherwise the price list's matching product or category rule; otherwise the base (or tier) price. The same calculation runs on every channel.

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → How a line's price is worked out.

# Refunds post as a journal entry linked to the credit memo

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: QuickBooks
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** A refund posts as a journal entry from the refund account to the customer's balance, linked to the credit memo.

**Turned down:** A Refund Receipt, which would credit the customer a second time.

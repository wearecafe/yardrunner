# YardRunner is the only sales channel, with no Shopify

Status: confirmed
Date: 2026-10-26
Area: Commerce
Decided by: Jon Moore

**Decision:** YardRunner is the yard's only sales channel and point of sale. Every goods sale and rental is quoted, priced, taxed, paid for and fulfilled in YardRunner. There is no Shopify integration.

**Turned down:** A Shopify push-only connector (built first, then removed).

**Source:** `docs/claude/commerce-data-model.md` → Decisions (Shopify).
